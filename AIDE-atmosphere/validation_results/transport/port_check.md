Causes of the remaining differences:

- jcm-strat teq_score.py takes calendar-month means of the 6-hourly frames by their timestamp (00Z on day 1 to 18Z on the last day). The port takes them from the daily means, and jcm-strat daily_zm.py builds each day from 06Z, 12Z, 18Z and 00Z of the next day. Every month therefore loses the 00Z frame that opens it and gains the one that opens the next month: one frame in about 120. The ERA5 side is identical. The shift table above estimates the effect from the daily series; it has the sign and size of every C2 difference. Open as AIDE-eval issue #7 (group C daily-mean input).
- A jet latitude is a grid latitude (argmax). Where two grid rows carry nearly the same wind, the one-frame shift can move the maximum by one row (1.87 deg). The tie table above lists the wind at both rows for every latitude that differs.
